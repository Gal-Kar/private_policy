**Effective Date:** September 29, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Japan Guide app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

## Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as:
* Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
* The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
* The time spent on the Application
* The operating system you use on your mobile device

The Application requires you to provide certain personally identifiable information to function properly, including but not limited to:
* **Authentication Data:** We use Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication to securely manage your account.
* **Location Data:** The Application collects your location data to provide map features and show nearby places using Google Maps and Google Places APIs. This data is only collected if you grant the necessary permissions.
* **Biometric Data:** The Application may use device-level biometric authentication (e.g., fingerprint or face unlock) for secure access. Biometric data is stored securely on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Your preferences and saved places may be stored in a local database (Room Database) on your device. Some user data may also be synced to our secure cloud database (Firebase Firestore) to allow you to access your information across multiple devices.
